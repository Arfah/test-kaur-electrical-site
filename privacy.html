Privacy policy

For client review before going live. This is a standard UK GDPR template for a website that collects no personal data directly. Simran should check the contact details, confirm how long enquiry records are kept, and confirm the ICO registration status before this page is published.

Last updated: September 2026.

Kaur Electrical is the trading name of Simran Kaur, a sole trader based in Maidenhead, Berkshire. This policy explains what personal data I collect, why, and what your rights are.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. This website has no contact form, no accounts, no cookies set by me, and no analytics. You can read every page without giving me any information.

The contact page embeds a Google Map. Google may set cookies or collect data when that map loads. This is governed by Google's privacy policy. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser requesting files from Google's servers.

What I collect when you contact me

When you phone, WhatsApp or email me, I receive whatever you send: usually your name, phone number, email address, the property address and a description of the electrical work you need.

I use this to reply, quote, book and carry out the work, and to issue certificates and invoices. The lawful basis is that this is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and then to perform that contract.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: deleted within 12 months.
  • Quotes, invoices and job records: kept for 6 years, as required for tax purposes.
  • Electrical certificates and reports (EICRs, installation certificates): kept for the period required by the NICEIC and the relevant regulations, so copies can be reissued if you lose yours.

Who I share it with

I do not sell or pass on your details. The only sharing is what the work requires: the NICEIC for certificate registration, Building Control where Part P notification applies, the OZEV scheme for eligible EV charger installs, my accountant for tax records, and my insurer if a claim arises.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask me to show you the data I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Email simran@kaurelectrical.co.uk or call 01628 570 118. If you are not happy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
